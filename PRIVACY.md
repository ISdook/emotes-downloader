# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

This covers the Emotes Downloader bot ("ISdook - emotes downloader"), the dashboard website at https://emotes-downloader.isdook.workers.dev, and the "Log in with Discord" feature of the Emotes Downloader app.

## What we store

- When you log in with Discord we store your Discord user ID, name and avatar, and your Discord login tokens (encrypted). We use them only to show you your own servers and to check your permissions.
- When you add, rename or delete an emoji or sticker with Emotes Downloader, we keep a log for 90 days: the server ID, your user ID and name, the emoji name, when it happened, and whether it worked. Server staff can see who added what.

## What we don't do

- We don't read messages.
- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't use tracking cookies. The only cookie is your login.

## Deleting your data

- Log out to delete your login right away. Logins you don't use for 30 days are deleted automatically.
- To remove everything, kick the bot from your server and open an issue on GitHub.
